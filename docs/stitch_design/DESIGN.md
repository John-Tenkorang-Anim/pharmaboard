## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, statutory, and audit-grade interface engineered specifically for national pharmaceutical oversight bodies, accredited health facilities, and regulatory officers. The visual language conveys non-negotiable compliance, uncompromised integrity, and operational clarity. It avoids casual, decorative SaaS tropes in favor of an institutional, high-precision technical console where every interaction leaves a clear record and every document status is unquestionable.

The design philosophy combines **Modern Corporate Institutionalism** with **Clinical Precision**. High-density data layouts, systematic structural borders, and unambiguous status tokens eliminate ambiguity during mission-critical tasks such as statutory drug recalls, practitioner licensing, facility accreditations, and legislative gazette dispatches.

### Target Audience & Operational Mindset
- **Regulatory Officers & Inspectors:** Require high scanability, rapid keyboard navigation, uncompromising audit metadata, and bulletproof approval workflows.
- **Accredited Facility Administrators:** Expect transparent compliance statuses, deterministic receipt acknowledgments, and clear action pathways for directives.
- **Council Leadership & Legal Counsel:** Require executive audit trails, cryptographically verified approval states, and tamper-evident logs.

### Visual Character
- **Statutory Authority:** Deep, unyielding nautical navy anchoring headers and primary control surfaces.
- **Clinical Rigor:** Crisp, high-contrast structural borders separating data panes without visual clutter.
- **Deterministic State Signaling:** Color is used functionally to encode statutory lifecycle states: validated compliance (teal/emerald), pending review (amber), and punitive or emergency enforcement (regulatory crimson).

## Colors

The color palette reflects statutory governance, trust, and clinical urgency. Defaulting strictly to a high-contrast light mode, the system preserves maximum legibility across clinical desktop displays and high-ambient lighting settings in hospital administrative wards.

### Primary Regulatory Palette
- **Regulatory Deep Navy (`#0A1E3F`):** Used for top-level council navigation, statutory headers, authoritative actions, and verified seal backgrounds. Communicates institutional permanence.
- **Council Slate Navy (`#0F274A`):** Used for primary interactive buttons, selected navigation states, and data table master headers.

### Specialized Accents & Verification Tones
- **Institutional Emerald / Teal (`#0D9488` / `#059669`):** Represents verified Council compliance, cryptographic digital signatures, valid accreditations, and successful statutory dispatches.
- **Regulatory Warning Amber (`#D97706`):** Indicates pending two-person approvals, approaching licensing expiration windows, or mandatory re-inspection flags.
- **Statutory Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Reserved exclusively for high-consequence events: Class I Drug Recalls, license revocations, unaccredited facility cease-and-desist orders, and failed audit checks.

### Neutral Foundation & Surfaces
- **Console Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary background ensuring low eye fatigue during extended audit reviews.
- **Container Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for table containers, inspection reports, and modal viewports.
- **Sub-Tier Surface (`#F1F5F9`):** Subdued canvas for audit trail timelines, disabled state blocks, and telemetry track beds.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px borders enforcing sharp compartmentalization between data fields.
- **High-Density Divider (`#CBD5E1`):** Applied between table rows, sticky table headers, and split-pane dividers.
- **Body Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Maximum contrast charcoal for critical legal copy, batch IDs, and statutory text.
- **Body Text Secondary (`#475569`):** Slate for timestamps, practitioner reference numbers, and non-critical table metadata.

## Typography

**Public Sans** is chosen as the exclusive typographic engine across all headlines, body copy, and UI metadata. Developed specifically for high-trust governmental and institutional platforms, Public Sans provides structured geometric stability, narrow vertical metrics, and distinct character glyphs (such as the distinct `1`, `I`, and `l`) essential for error-free reading of pharmaceutical licenses, NDC codes, and dispatch IDs.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Usage Rules
- **Headline XL / LG:** Applied exclusively to page-level statutory titles, official dispatch manifests, and council resolution document views.
- **Headline MD / SM:** Used for panel headings, table widget titles, and multi-tier modal prompts.
- **Body MD:** The default reading size for regulatory text, legislative commentary, and directive descriptions.
- **Body SM:** Standard density sizing for dense data grid cells, metadata lists, and secondary narrative entries.
- **Label MD / SM:** Set in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for status badges, column headers, security classifications, and authorization badges.
- **Tabular Figures:** All numeric data displayed in audit logs, telemetry metrics, and financial or dosage parameters must utilize OpenType tabular lining figures (`tnum`) to ensure perfect vertical alignment in dense tables.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 8px base rhythm with a sub-4px micro-increment scale (`0.25rem` / 4px and `0.125rem` / 2px) to support data-dense administrative consoles without sacrificing structure.

### Grid & Viewport Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid grid system with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins. Layout features a fixed 260px administrative left rail, expandable secondary filter sidebars (320px), and an infinite or paginated primary data table container.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. The primary navigation collapses to an authoritative top bar with an off-canvas drawer; multi-step regulatory wizards compress from multi-column split forms to single-column sequential stacks.
- **Mobile Handheld (<768px):** 4-column layout with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` safe margins. Table views pivot to dense key-value inspection cards, preserving cryptographic badges and action triggers at the viewport bottom.

### Density & Vertical Rhythm
- **Data Table Rows:** Standard regulatory row height is calibrated to `40px` (dense) and `48px` (default) to facilitate high-volume data auditing without scrolling fatigue.
- **Inspection Panes:** Audit detail drawers maintain a fixed 24px internal padding, utilizing `0.75rem` inter-item gaps for metadata pairings (Label directly over Value).

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain institutional authority and prevent visual float, this design system rejects heavy or stylized drop shadows. Spatial depth is established through **structural layering**, **crisp boundary borders**, and **low-contrast neutral elevation**.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Base canvas behind all data modules and consoles. Completely flat.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Containers & Tables):** `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed in a continuous `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. No shadow is applied; isolation is achieved purely through color contrast against the `#F8FAFC` canvas.
3. **Level 2 (Active Focus & Sticky Elements):** Table header strips, pinned regulatory filter rails, and hovering action menus use a targeted low-contrast outline combined with a crisp institutional shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Regulatory Drawers & Detail Flyouts):** Used for single-record audit logs and verification panels:
   `box-shadow: -4px 0 16px 0 rgba(10, 30, 63, 0.08)`.
5. **Level 4 (Statutory Modals & Security Overlays):** Used for irreversible actions (e.g., Two-Person Approval signings, nationwide dispatch release):
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 30, 63, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 30, 63, 0.08)`.
   Backed by a semi-opaque modal scrim: `rgba(10, 30, 63, 0.65)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric specification to balance modern UI expectations with administrative sobriety. Curvature is kept modest to reflect institutional documentation and physical regulatory seals.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Micro UI Elements (Badges, Checkboxes, Inline Tags):** `2px` border radius (`rounded-xs`).
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors, Tabs):** `4px` border radius (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem`). This prevents bubbly, informal silhouettes and reinforces technical precision.
- **Card Containers, Data Tables, & Modal Shells:** `8px` border radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`).
- **Pills / Status Pointers:** `9999px` is strictly forbidden for standard UI controls; it is reserved exclusively for high-visibility circular indicator pips and count indicators embedded inside table tab labels.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Statutory Action:** Solid Council Slate Navy (`#0F274A`) with white text and `4px` border radius. On hover: `#0A1E3F`. Focus ring: `2px solid #0D9488` with `2px` offset.
- **Critical Enforcement Action:** Solid Statutory Crimson (`#DC2626`) with white text. Reserved for recalls, suspensions, and access revocation. On hover: `#B91C1C`.
- **Secondary / Audited Action:** Pure white background, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, text `#0F172A`. On hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#0A1E3F` text and border.
- **Icon Buttons:** Fixed 32x32px or 36x36px squares with `4px` radius, housing 16px technical icons. Used for table export, log print, and record filtering.

### Verification Badges & Status Indicators
Badges are high-contrast, structured labels featuring a 1px border, uppercase bold typography (`label-sm`), and a 6px status pip:
- **Verified Council Seal:** Background `#F0FDFA`, border `#99F6E4`, text `#0F766E`. Left-aligned with an official crest or verified check mark.
- **Two-Person Approved:** Dual-state pill. Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#0F274A`, text `#0A1E3F`. Displays signature glyphs for Approver A and Approver B.
- **Critical Priority (Recall/Alert):** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`, text `#991B1B`.
- **Auditable Lifecycle Indicators:** 
  - *Provider Accepted:* Cyan-tinted Slate (`#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1`, text `#334155`).
  - *Stored / Archived:* Deep Neutral Slate (`#F8FAFC`, text `#64748B`).
  - *Read / Opened:* Indigo-Slate (`#EEF2FF`, border `#C7D2FE`, text `#3730A3`).
  - *Acknowledged by Facility:* Emerald (`#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`).

### Data Tables & Audit Grids
- **Header:** Background `#0A1E3F` with crisp white uppercase text (`label-sm`, tracking `0.05em`) or dense neutral `#F1F5F9` with slate text (`#334155`). Sticky positioning with a bottom border of `2px solid #CBD5E1`.
- **Row Styling:** Alternate background rows (Zebra: `#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC`) with a persistent `1px solid #E2E8F0` divider. Hovering highlights the full row in `#F0FDF4` (if verified) or `#F1F5F9` (standard).
- **Cell Density:** Compact 8px top/bottom padding, 12px left/right padding. Numeric cells right-aligned in tabular lining font.

### Form Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selectors:** `36px` height, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `4px` radius. Focus: border color shifts to `#0A1E3F` with an external teal ring (`#0D9488`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square/round controls with a `1.5px` perimeter. Checked state displays `#0A1E3F` background with an institutional white tick.
- **Validation State:** Persistent error text in `#DC2626` anchored directly below the field, paired with a `1.5px solid #DC2626` input border.

### Telemetry & Auditing Cards
- **Statutory Metric Tiles:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `4px` radius. Top accent border (2px height) colored by state (Navy = Active, Teal = Compliant, Crimson = Breached). Displays large tabular numeric readouts alongside an immutable timestamp.
- **Security Action Dialog:** Full-screen modal overlay with a fixed 540px width. Features an authoritative navy header band, legal compliance warning statement, two-factor / cryptographic pin field, and dual side-by-side verification confirmation buttons.
