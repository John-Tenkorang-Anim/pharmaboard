# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-25

## Decision

Material decisions are recorded in `docs/adr/` using context, decision, consequences, and measurable revisit conditions. Accepted ADRs are immutable; a later decision supersedes rather than rewrites them.

## Consequence

Reviewers can distinguish intentional constraints from omissions, and the project can evolve without losing why a choice was made.
